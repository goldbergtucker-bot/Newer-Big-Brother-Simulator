Big Brother 20 Twists

BB App Store
Weeks 1–3

Each week, Houseguests receive either a Power App or a Crap App based on the simulated App Store trend results. A Houseguest who receives an app becomes ineligible to receive another app.

Power Apps

  • Bonus Life — gives an eligible evicted Houseguest a chance to compete to return to the game.
  • The Cloud — protects its holder from one nomination or veto ceremony.
  • Identity Theft — allows its holder to secretly replace both of the HOH's nominations.

Crap Apps

  • Hamazon — recurring ham-delivery punishment.
  • Yell! — surprise angry-reviewer feedback for 24 hours.
  • Read It! — requires the Houseguest to perform/read Hamlet in costume and character.
H@cker Competition
Weeks 6–7

An anonymous H@cker is selected through a competition.

  • Replace one nominee.
  • Choose one of the three randomly selected Veto participants.
  • Nullify one eviction vote.

The simulator applies these powers through the weekly game engine.

Jury Battle Back
After the first 4 jurors

The first four Jury members compete in Big Top Drop for a chance to return to the game.

Double Eviction
Week 11

Week 11 contains a second rapid-fire HOH, nomination, POV and eviction cycle.
