Big Brother 20 format, twists and competitions.

Big Brother 20 Twists

BB App Store
Weeks 1–3

Houseguests compete for special powers and punishments through the BB App Store.

Apps

  • Bonus Life — Gives an evicted Houseguest an opportunity to return.
  • Hamazon — A punishment involving food deliveries.
  • The Cloud — A power that can protect a Houseguest from nomination.
  • Yell! — A punishment/power involving an interruption.
  • Identity Theft — Allows a Houseguest to alter the nomination ceremony.
  • Read It! — A punishment involving reading.
H@cker Competition
Weeks 6–7

The winner receives special control over the week's nominations, Power of Veto players and eviction vote.

H@cker Powers

  • Replace one nominee.
  • Select one Houseguest to participate in the POV.
  • Nullify one eviction vote.
Jury Battle Back
Jury Phase

The first four eligible jury members compete for a chance to return to the game.

Battle Back Competition

  • Big Top Drop
  • Four eligible jury members participate.
  • Winner returns to the game.
Double Eviction
Week 11

The second eviction occurs immediately after the first eviction with a new HOH and POV cycle.
